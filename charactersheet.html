Rimworld TTRPG

Character Sheet

Overview

Me and my friends wanted to play a TTRPG set in the video game of “Rimworld”, however, no system I looked at captured the game’s feel.

Therefore, the only sensible response was to create my own tabletop system. However; because it was a new system it meant that no existing character sheet for other games would suit the player’s needs, which lead to me creating my own to accommodate the new mechanics such as mood, weapon profiles and companions.
