Production

This team’s development timeline was hosted on Jira where the producer and department leads would post the relevant tasks within each sprint. Due to Jira’s nature, it allowed the team to properly visualise the scrums which reduced a lot of the confusion on what needed to be done to reach certain goals.

Design

The driving force of the User Interface was to keep the HUD as minimal as possible.

The main reason for this was due of the tactility of the minigames it meant that the player’s cursor would be moving around the HUD constantly. This is why the HUD swaps out to a "Minigame HUD" whenever the player enters a workstation; so that they don’t confuse which UI elements are guiding them through the minigame and which aren’t.
