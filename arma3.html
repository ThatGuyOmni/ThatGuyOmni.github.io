ARMA 3

Flags and Emblems

Overview

I’m a part of an Arma 3 group, and one of the things I do in the group is create emblems and flags for the different squads. I work directly with a squad lead to create a design that encapsulates their team, I work with them personally to create a brief then send over several drafts and iterations so we can collaborate together on the project.

It’s a really nice feeling to work on something and then see it in the game you play.
