Production

Due to the short timeline on this project the team decided on a hybrid production method by starting with a waterfall and ending with an agile methodology. In practice; this took the form of having the first two days being a waterfall so that a playable prototype could be completed as soon as possible, with the last day being reserved for iteration cycles to add some polish to the product.

Design

A huge focus of this project was the accessibility of not only the UI but the game as a whole. Resources such as “The Accessible Games Initiative” and other accessibility frameworks helped guide the team’s decisions throughout the project, namely the inclusion of “Opendyslexic” as the game’s default font provides a better experience for those with dyslexia.

Reflection

This project was completed as a part of a game jam that was hosted Tencent and managed to get first place within the final judging.

In the timeframe a small vertical slice was completed which abides to both the jam theme of accessibility and the team’s initial brief of “pick-up and play” casual fun. As a whole the product is very unpolished, however, still does an excellent job at providing numerous features that improve the experience of those with different types of impairments.

After the Jam was completed, I wanted to gain some User Research experience, so I created a User Research Doc, where I completed a moderated user test and had participants fill out a survey using Useberry.
