I’m Kit, a game designer that specialises in UI/UX

My areas of interest are in user-centred design and accessibility, as my motivation is to make products that are accessibile and enjoyable to all. The interface is the voice of the developer and if it is not clear then it is not heard, and I create this clarity though collaboration with others as my perspective is not all seeing and not without its blind spots.

Besides designing games; I’m an avid tabletop fan whether that’s wargaming or role playing games, I also partake in a few outdoor hobbies such as hiking to try and mitigate all of the time my face is pressed against a screen.
